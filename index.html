Frequently Asked Questions

Do you prepare tax returns outside tax season?
Yes. The firm offers services throughout the year. Contact us to discuss your needs and arrange an appointment.
Can I submit my tax documents online?
The firm offers a secure client portal for document uploads. Contact us if you need help accessing it.
Can you help with past-due tax returns or tax debt concerns?
The firm lists services relating to prior-year returns, amended filings, tax liens, and certain tax debt matters. We'll discuss your situation to determine whether we can assist.
Do you serve clients outside Washington?
The firm states that it prepares federal and state tax returns nationwide. Contact us to confirm availability for your particular needs.
How much do tax preparation services cost?
Fees depend on the service and complexity of your situation. Contact us to discuss pricing and request an estimate where available.
What if you cannot help with my tax issue?
The firm indicates that it will assess whether your needs fall within its scope and, where possible, direct you toward another suitable professional.
